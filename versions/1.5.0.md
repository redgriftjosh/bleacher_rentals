### 💵 Setup and teardown pay now follows each driver

**Team →** open a driver **→ Driver tab → Manager Setup.** Admins can now set
flat **Setup Pay** and **Teardown Pay** amounts alongside the driver's deadhead
rate.

**Work Trackers →** open a trip. When **Setup Required** or **Teardown Required**
is selected, the matching **Line Items** row now uses that driver's saved rate.
The calculator beside **Driver Pay** sets the payment to the full line-items total,
including hauling, deadhead, setup, teardown and any custom work.
