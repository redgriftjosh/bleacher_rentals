### 💵 See every part of a driver's pay on the trip

**Work Trackers →** open a trip **→ Line Items** tab. You can now itemize hauling,
deadhead, setup, teardown, maintenance, per diem and custom work with a quantity,
rate, description and total.

- **Hauling and deadhead calculate automatically** from the route and selected
  driver's rates.
- Checking **Setup Required** or **Teardown Required** on the **Details** tab adds
  the matching line; unchecking it removes that line.
- Set each driver's deadhead rate under **Team →** open the driver **→ Driver** tab
  **→ Manager Setup → Deadhead Rate**.

---

### 🚚 Fix trips without stopping the driver's progress

Account managers can now edit or move trips that are in progress, completed or
cancelled. Open the **work tracker modal** to correct the details, or drag the trip
on the **Dashboard schedule grid**. An in-progress trip keeps its current status,
and the assigned driver is notified when a change affects them.
