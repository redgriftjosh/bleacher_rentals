### 📊 Ask your drivers how it's going, and see the answers

**Driver Satisfaction** is a new page, beside the two scorecards in the sidebar.

Drivers are asked one question in the mobile app — how satisfied they are
overall, out of ten — and anyone scoring 6 or below is asked why in their own
words. Nobody is asked on a schedule: a driver is asked after their first
completed trip, then again once enough days have passed since they last answered.
Someone who has been away for months comes back to **one** question, not a queue
of them.

The page shows the **average score**, how many drivers **need a follow-up**, how
many are **promoters**, and how many drivers you have **heard from** at all.
Below that, a trend over time and the full list of responses. Change the period
or filter by score and everything moves together.

Account managers and viewers can read all of it.

### 🎫 Drivers can report problems without leaving the app

A driver who hits a problem can now file a ticket straight from the mobile app.
It lands in **Roadmap → Backlog** like any other, and the card says who sent it,
so you know whether you are reading a manager's request or a driver's.

They can tidy up their own ticket for a short while after sending it. They see
nothing else on the roadmap.

### ⚡ The roadmap saves as you go

**Roadmap →** clicking **+ New Feature**, **+ New Task** or **+ Submit Ticket**
now creates the record straight away and opens it for editing. No more filling in
a blank form and losing it if something goes wrong.

Edits save themselves as you type, with an indicator showing when the last one
landed. If a save fails — a dropped connection, usually — it tells you instead of
pretending. Deleting is a soft delete, so a record removed by mistake is
recoverable.

### 📄 Driver documents: look before you download

**Team →** open a driver **→ Driver tab.** Licences, insurance and medical cards
can be previewed in place rather than downloaded first, and each now takes an
**expiry date** — so an out-of-date document can actually be spotted.
