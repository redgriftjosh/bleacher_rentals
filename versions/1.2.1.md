### 🔄 More reliable startup when you have several tabs open

When you open the app in more than one Chrome tab — including after your browser
restores tabs — the local data service now starts reliably instead of leaving the
app on its loading screen.

This change was prompted by a real incident where the app got stuck on an infinite
loading screen and required switching to a different browser and clearing cached
data to recover. To prevent this from happening again, the underlying local data
service has been updated to use a more broadly compatible storage approach. This
comes with a modest tradeoff in raw startup speed, but the result is a much more
predictable and stable experience — especially after browser updates or when
multiple tabs are involved.
