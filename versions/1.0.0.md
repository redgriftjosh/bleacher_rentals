### 💬 Account managers can now do this without an admin

- **Send and revoke team invites** — **Team** page, invite button
- **Finish setting up half-created users** (invited, no role assigned yet) —
  **Team → All** tab, open the user
- **Assign drivers to zones**, including adding yourself as a driver — **Team →**
  open a user **→ Driver** tab, zone picker
- **Load QuickBooks sales tax when building a quote** — **Quotes & Bookings → New
  Quote**, the **Tax** line in the totals panel

Invite failures on the **Team** page now say what actually went wrong ("this
email already exists", "badly formatted", "too many attempts") instead of
"Failed to invite user".

### 📄 Quotes

All on the **public quote page** — the link you send the client.

- **Clients can't sign a quote that changed underneath them.** Edit a quote they
  have open and their page shows _"This quote has been updated"_ within ~10
  seconds. If they try to sign from the **Signed Contract** tab anyway, the
  server re-checks the terms at signing time and refuses. Previewing your own
  quote while signed in won't trigger any of this.
- **The page works on a phone.** Clients open these on mobile constantly.
- **Clearer payment wording** on the **Pay Invoice** tab — "Due Now" / "Due on
  \<date\>" / "Final Due on \<date\>", plus a note that clients can **pay by ACH
  or check**, not just card.

### 🚚 Scheduling

- **Moving an accepted trip now asks first.** Dragging a work tracker on the
  **Dashboard** grid that a driver already accepted drops it back to Released and
  makes them re-accept — you now get a **"Move Accepted Trip?"** confirmation
  before that happens.
- Drivers only get notified for changes that affect them. In the **work tracker
  modal**: date, driver, address, time and pay changes un-accept and notify;
  notes-only changes just notify; minor edits stay quiet.
- **Distance tooltip** — **Dashboard → Options → Show Distance Tooltip**. Hover a
  bleacher cell to see the drive between its last and next location
  (`264.8 mi (426.1 km)`). Turning it on turns **Show Address Tooltip** off; only
  one can be active.

### 🔒 Security

- **Payment records are locked down.** The payment history table (payer names,
  emails, amounts, receipts) was reachable through the API. It isn't anymore. No
  customer data is known to have been accessed.
- **Quote links are no longer guessable** — they used the sequential invoice
  number, so someone could walk the list and read other customers' quotes.

⚠️ **Old quote links stop working.** No client links have been sent yet, so this
affects nobody in practice — but re-copy any quote URL you saved in a note or
draft email.

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Your own driver-zone assignment no longer vanishes on save** — **Team →** your
  user **→ Driver** tab. It said "User updated successfully" and silently threw
  the zone away.
- **Damage reports can't be marked resolved without a repair.** In the damage
  report on **Quality Assurance → Damage Reports**, the resolved toggle is gone,
  replaced by **Create Maintenance to Resolve** — so a closed report always points
  at real work.
- **One Linxup device can now be tracked on multiple bleachers** — **Assets →
  Bleachers →** the **Linxup Device** field.
- **Dashboard zone filtering fixed** — the zone selector beside **Options**.
