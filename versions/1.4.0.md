### 📸 Damage report photos: smaller uploads, iPhone photos just work

**Damage Reports page →** create or edit a report. Photos are now resized and
compressed before upload, and iPhone HEIC photos are converted automatically —
no more "invalid file type" errors when a driver's photo comes straight off
their phone.

- Up to **30 photos** per report.
- A damage severity, a note, and at least one photo are now required before
  you can save a report.

---

### 🕓 Reports no longer show up before their photos are ready

**Damage Reports page.** A report created over a weak signal used to appear
in the list right away, even while its photos were still uploading in the
background — looking incomplete or broken. New reports now stay hidden until
every photo is confirmed uploaded.

- Developers can click **Hiding Uploading Photos** (top-right) to reveal
  reports that are still mid-upload for troubleshooting.
