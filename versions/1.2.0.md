### 💰 Driver pay by distance, not one flat rate

**Team →** open a driver **→ Driver** tab **→ Manager Setup**. The old **Pay
Rate** box is gone. In its place is a line running from **0** to **∞**, showing
what the driver earns across the whole trip length:

```
0MI ———— $3.00/MI ———— 100MI ———— $2.50/MI ———— 200MI ———— $2.25/MI ———— ∞MI
```

[Watch it in action](https://www.loom.com/share/ef3d4570b73f469990e31145ca3e7d77)

- **Click the line** to add a cutoff. It asks for the value, in that driver's unit.
- **Click a rate** to change it.
- **Click a cutoff** to move it, or remove it — removing merges the two ranges
  either side.

A driver with no ranges is one unbroken line showing a single rate, exactly like
the old box. Nothing to set up unless you want tiers.

**Currency**, **Per Unit** and **Tax** stay where they were, above the line.
Cutoffs are in whatever **Per Unit** says — KM, MI or HR — so switching the unit
switches what the numbers mean.

**Who can edit:** admins, and account managers who share a zone with the driver
— the same rule that already governs the rest of that driver's details.

### 🧮 The pay calculator uses those ranges, and shows its work

**Work Trackers →** open a trip **→** the **calculator icon** beside the **Pay**
box. It now picks the rate for the distance actually travelled instead of always
using the base rate. **Hover it** before clicking and it spells out the sum:

![The pay calculator tooltip showing the distance, rate and total](/changelog/1.2.0-calculate-pay-screenshot.png)

One rate covers the whole trip — the range the trip lands in. A 150-mile trip in
a **100–200 @ $2.50** range is 150 × $2.50, not part-priced at each tier below
it.

Drivers with no ranges calculate exactly as they always did, at their base rate —
as does a trip that falls outside every range you've set.
