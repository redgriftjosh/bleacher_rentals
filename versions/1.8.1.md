### 🍁 Canadian quotes now work out their own tax

**Quotes & Bookings →** create or edit a quote **→ Totals.** A quote for an event
in Canada now fills in the right tax on its own — 13% in Ontario, 14.975% in
Quebec, 5% in Alberta, and so on for every province and territory. Until now the
tax line stayed empty for Canadian addresses and somebody had to know the rate
and type it in.

The rate follows the **event address**, not the sales office, which is how
Canadian tax actually works: what you charge depends on where the bleachers end
up. Quebec's rate runs to three decimal places, and the tax line shows all of
them rather than rounding.

### 🚧 A quote can't straddle the border

If the **Sales Office** and the **event address** are in different countries — a
Canadian office with an American event address, or the other way round — the
quote now says so in red rather than quietly leaving the tax blank. Pick an
office in the same country as the event, or enter the tax yourself.

### ✉️ A warning before a quote can't be sent

**Quotes & Bookings →** create or edit a quote **→ Account Manager.** Picking
someone whose email is not a **@bleacherrentals.com** address now shows a warning
under the dropdown.

Quotes go out from the account manager's own address, so a personal one can't
send at all. Better to find that out while filling in the quote than at the
moment you press send.
