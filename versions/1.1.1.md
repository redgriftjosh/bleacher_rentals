### 📄 Shorter company address on the quote you send clients

On the **public quote page** — the link clients open — the company address
is now a single line of **street and ZIP**. It shows up in two spots: the
letterhead at the top, and the **Make checks payable to:** box beside the
**Totals** panel. Both used to wrap onto a second line carrying the city
and state.

Nothing is lost — the street line already carries the city and state, so
that second line was repeating them.
