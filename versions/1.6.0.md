### 🇫🇷 Quotes can now go out in French

**Companies & Contacts →** open a contact **→ Edit → Quote Language.** Set a
contact to French and everything they receive arrives in French: the quote page,
the contract they sign, the payment screen and the PDF. The same dropdown sits on
the new-contact form, including the one inside the quote builder.

Dates and amounts follow Canadian French convention — `15 janv. 2026` and
`1 234,56 $`. English quotes are untouched.

Terms & Conditions are not translated for you: write a French T&C document and
select it on the quote as usual.

Clients can also fix the language themselves from the globe icon at the top of
their quote — useful when a contact was set up wrong. That switch shows up in
**Quote → Log → Customer Activity**, so you know to correct the contact record.

### 🚚 Drivers can report which bleacher they actually took

When the assigned bleacher is buried at the back of the yard, drivers take an
equivalent one from the front. They can now say so when submitting an inspection,
with a reason — blocked, damaged, hard to get to, or not on site.

**You get a push notification** naming the trip, the bleacher taken and why.
Inspections and damage reports are filed against the bleacher that actually went
out, not the one on paper.

The Bill of Lading and bleacher availability still show the bleacher you
assigned. Reconciling the two stays your call — the notification is the prompt.

### 📇 Point of contact fills itself in on work trackers

**Work Trackers →** open a trip **→ Details.** Pickup and Dropoff POC are now
real contact records instead of typed-in text, so the phone number travels with
the name. The locate button beside each pulls the POC from the booked event or
the neighbouring trip — the same way the address locate button already works.

If the neighbouring trip only has a typed-in name with no contact behind it, the
button tells you to create the contact first rather than copying a name with no
number attached.

### ⚡ Create a work tracker straight from the dashboard

**Dashboard →** ⌘+click an empty cell (Ctrl+click on Windows) to open a new work
tracker for that bleacher and date, skipping the popup. The truck button in the
cell popup still works. Both run the same permission checks, so you can still
only create trackers on bleachers that are yours.

### 🧭 Fixes

**Work Trackers →** the driver list now follows zones. You see the drivers who
share a zone with you, matching how the rest of the app already works. Admins and
the **See All Drivers** toggle still show everyone.

**Companies & Contacts →** contact and company forms now catch bad email
addresses and phone numbers, flagging a field only once you've left it. Save
stays disabled until the required fields are filled.
