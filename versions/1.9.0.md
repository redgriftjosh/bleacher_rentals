### 💵 Record a payment that never went through Stripe

**Quotes & Bookings →** open a quote **→ Billing tab → + Record Payment.** The
button used to be greyed out. Now it opens a dialog for a check, an ACH transfer
or a card run by hand on a terminal, with the date, the payer, a reference number
and a note.

A recorded payment can never be edited or deleted. A mistake — a bounced check, a
refund, a wrong amount — is corrected by recording the same amount as a
**negative**, so both entries stay on the record and the history reads like a
ledger. Recording a refund here does not send money back through Stripe; that is
still done in Stripe.

Administrators and lead account managers can record a payment on any quote. Other
account managers can do it on the quotes they created. Viewers cannot, but they
can still read everything.

### 🔍 The payment history now says what actually happened

**Billing tab.** Amounts come from the money that actually arrived rather than
from a paid/unpaid flag, so an installment that has received part of what it is
owed now shows as **partial** instead of jumping straight to paid. Clicking any
payment opens the full record — every installment it was applied to, the
reference, the notes and who entered it.

The client's own **Pay** tab is worked out the same way, so the balance they see
and the balance you see cannot disagree.

### ✍️ Paying an installment no longer invalidates the signature

A client who paid an installment was immediately asked to re-sign a contract they
had already signed, and was shown a "this quote has been updated" notice for a
payment they had just made themselves. Nothing about the agreement had changed —
only money had arrived. Both are fixed.

While we were in there, the client's Pay tab now says an amount is **"Due"**
rather than **"Overdue"**, which is what it usually is.

### 📊 Subtotal and tax, side by side on the list

**Quotes & Bookings.** The single **Amount** column is now **Subtotal** and
**Tax**, each with its own total in the column header. Canadian and American
quotes are totalled separately rather than added together, and every figure is
shown in the currency the quote is actually priced in.

Long event names now shorten cleanly with the full name on hover, instead of
being chopped off mid-word.

### 🏢 Filter by sales office

**Quotes & Bookings → Filters.** A new **Sales Office** dropdown narrows the list
to one office. "All Sales Offices" clears it in one click.

Office names in the quote form now carry the currency they actually sell in —
`Ontario Office (CAD)`. An office whose address is missing a province used to be
offered as USD while the quote it produced was priced and charged in CAD.

### 📒 Mark a quote as invoiced in QuickBooks

**Billing tab → QuickBooks.** A **QuickBooks Invoice** checkbox records by hand
that an event has been invoiced. It is bookkeeping only — nothing is pushed to
QuickBooks — and the change is logged, so you can see who ticked it and when.

Administrators and account managers can set it on any quote, including ones they
did not create — bookkeeping is not the quote owner's job. Viewers can see it but
not change it.

### 📝 Two smaller things on a new quote

**+ Create Quote** now starts with the standard delivery-window and interest
disclaimer already in **Client-Facing Notes**, so nobody has to remember to paste
it. A draft you had already started is left alone.

A blank quote can now be saved as a draft. Save no longer refuses because the
event name or dates are empty — it files it as an untitled draft for you to
finish later. Sending a quote still requires everything.

### ✉️ E-transfer instructions on the quote

The "make checks payable to" box on the quote a client sees now also gives the
e-transfer address, in English and in French.

### 🧹 Fixes

- **Event chat:** opening the chat on an event you own occasionally failed
  outright, and quietly put you back in a chat you had deliberately left. An
  owner who leaves now stays out, and rejoins with the **Join** button like
  anyone else.
- **Sidebar:** long section names no longer push the sidebar wide or spill past
  its edge.
