### 🇨🇦 Driver tax now supports Quebec's rate

**Manage Team → a driver → Driver section.** The tax rate field used to only
take whole percent, so Quebec's 14.975% got typed in and quietly stored as 14.
Every payout worked out from that driver's tax was short. The field now takes
decimals — type `14.975` and it stays `14.975`.

### 🔢 Work tracker quantities no longer round to whole units

**Work Trackers → a driver's week → Line Items tab.** A half day of setup or
2.5 hours of maintenance used to have to be rounded to a whole number before it
could be entered, so the pay for that line was off by up to half a unit's
worth. The quantity field now takes one decimal place, so `2.5` is `2.5`.

### 💵 Filter the weekly driver list by currency

**Work Trackers → a week.** A new **Currency** dropdown next to "See All
Drivers" narrows the list to CAD or USD drivers only. "All Currencies" clears
it.

### 🔗 Quotes & Bookings remembers your filters when you come back

**Quotes & Bookings.** Opening a quote and hitting the browser's back button
used to dump you back onto an empty, unfiltered list. Filters, the search box,
and Show Deleted now round-trip through the page's URL, so back restores the
list exactly as you left it — and the link itself can be copied or bookmarked
with those filters baked in.
