### 💳 Client payments now finish cleanly

Two problems were breaking the end of the payment flow. Both are fixed.

- **Paying no longer dumps the client on a broken page.** After a client hit
  **Pay** on the **public quote page → Pay Invoice** tab, Stripe sent them back
  to a link built from the invoice number — but in 1.0.0 those links moved to
  the secure quote link, so that page no longer existed. Clients got a **404
  instead of the green "Payment Successful!" banner**, which reads like the
  payment failed. They now land back on the **Pay Invoice** tab with the
  confirmation banner.

- **Payment History records payments again.** Stripe's webhook wasn't pointed at
  the app, so completed payments never wrote a row. The endpoint is corrected,
  and payments now show up with their receipt link in the **Payment History**
  section in both places it appears — **Quotes & Bookings →** open a quote **→
  Billing** tab for you, and the client's **Pay Invoice** tab for them.

The money always reached Stripe in both cases — what broke was the client's
confirmation and our record of it. **If any payment was taken before this
release, check it against Stripe**, since it may be missing from Payment
History.
