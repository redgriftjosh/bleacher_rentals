### ✍️ Clients can sign again after you edit a booked quote

**The big one.** If you edited a quote the client had already signed, their
**Signed Contract** tab on the **public quote page** kept showing "already
signed" — so they could never sign the revised terms, and the system couldn't
record a new signature either. You were stuck chasing it outside the app.

Editing contract terms now automatically retires the old signature and puts the
signing form back in front of the client.

- **Counts as a contract change:** price and line items, event dates, venue
  address, payment schedule, taxes, terms & conditions, sales office.
- **Doesn't:** client notes and PO number — edit those freely without making
  anyone re-sign.

The original signature is never deleted. It's a legal record, so the signature
and its PDF stay on file and are only marked as superseded.

### 💳 Payments

- **A real confirmation screen.** Paying on the **Pay Invoice** tab now lands the
  client on a dedicated success page with a **Back to Quote** button, instead of
  a banner buried on the tab they just left. Before this release it was a **404**
  — the redirect still used the old invoice-number link that moved in 1.0.0,
  which reads like the payment failed.
- **Payment History records payments again.** Stripe's webhook wasn't pointed at
  the app, so completed payments never wrote a row. Payments now appear with
  their receipt link in the **Payment History** section — **Quotes & Bookings →**
  open a quote **→ Billing** tab for you, and the client's **Pay Invoice** tab
  for them.
- **Payment notification emails are firing.** The payment-made templates for both
  the client and the account manager were inactive in **Email Automation**
  settings, so nothing sent after a successful payment. Both are configured and
  live now.

The money always reached Stripe throughout — what broke was the client's
confirmation and our record of it. **Check any payment taken before this release
against Stripe**, since it may be missing from Payment History.
