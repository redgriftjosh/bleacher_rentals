# Preflight — 1.9.0

PR: #372
Status: planned

The big one in this release is money: `+ Record Payment` on the Billing tab now
works, and every balance on the page — staff side and client side — is worked out
from the payments that actually arrived rather than from a paid/unpaid flag. The
same change fixes a client being asked to re-sign a contract just because they
paid an installment.

Most of it is already covered. The allocator, the amount-due maths, the tax and
subtotal columns, the sales-office filter, the draft defaults and the new-quote
notes all have unit tests; the RLS boundary has a psql test that proves a viewer
is refused and that nobody can ever update or delete a payment row; and there are
Playwright specs for recording a payment as an admin, as a lead AM and as a
viewer.

What is left below is what only a person can judge: that the numbers agree with
each other across three different screens, and that a real Stripe payment still
behaves once the code underneath it has been rewritten.

**One permission changed while writing this** — see the note at the end.

## New in this release

- [x] **Quotes & Bookings →** open a booking **→ Billing → + Record Payment.**
      Record a **check** for part of what is owed. The installment shows
      **partial** (not paid), the balance drops by exactly that amount, and the
      row names you as the person who recorded it.
- [x] Record a **negative** amount on the same quote — the dialog says out loud
      that it is a reversal before you send it, and the balance goes back up.
      Neither row offers any way to edit or delete it.
- [x] Click a payment row. The detail dialog shows every installment the money
      was applied to, the reference and the note, and the numbers add up to the
      row you clicked.
- [x] Open the **client's** quote link for that same booking **→ Pay tab.** The
      amount due matches the Billing tab exactly, and the wording says **"is
      due"**, not "is overdue".
- [x] **The signature fix.** On a quote that has been signed, pay or record an
      installment, then reload the client link. The contract tab still shows it
      as signed and does **not** ask for a new signature, and no "this quote has
      been updated" notice appears.
- [x] **Billing → QuickBooks Invoice.** Tick it on a quote **you did not
      create**, reload the page, and it is still ticked. Then check the change
      log shows who set it.
- [x] **Quotes & Bookings list.** The **Subtotal** and **Tax** columns show per
      quote, and the column headers total them. Filter to Canadian quotes only —
      the headers show C$ and are not mixed in with US dollars.
- [x] **Filters → Sales Office.** Pick one office; only its quotes remain, and
      the column totals move with the list. "All Sales Offices" clears it.
- [x] **+ Create Quote.** The delivery-window and interest disclaimer is already
      in Client-Facing Notes. Delete it, leave, come back to a draft in progress
      — it is not pasted back over your edit.
- [x] Press **Save** on a completely empty new quote. It saves as a draft rather
      than refusing, and the quote reopens with the fields still blank for you to
      finish.
- [x] Open the client link on a quote and confirm the **e-transfer address**
      appears in the "make checks payable to" box. (It is on the web page only,
      not the downloadable PDF — confirm that is what you want.)

## Could have broken

- [x] **A real Stripe payment still lands.** Pay a quote through the client link
      with a card. The payment appears on the Billing tab within a few seconds,
      the installment closes, and the balance is right. This is the path most
      disturbed by the release — the webhook no longer writes installment
      status at all.
- [x] **The payment schedule editor.** Edit a schedule on a quote that has
      already received money: an installment that has been paid cannot be
      deleted, and editing the others does not re-point the payment already
      recorded against them.
- [x] **The sidebar.** Every section still opens and closes, the page beside it
      is not pushed sideways, and no label spills past the edge — the sidebar was
      restyled and it is on every page.
- [x] **Event chat.** Open the chat on an event you own: it loads without an
      error. Leave the chat, reload — you are still out, and **Join** puts you
      back. The client-side auto-subscribe was removed here.

## Not testing

- **Who may record a payment, at the database level** — `manual_payment_entry.test.sql`
  proves an admin and an account manager may insert, a viewer may not, nobody may
  ever UPDATE or DELETE, zero is refused, negatives are allowed, and a client
  cannot forge a row claiming to have come from Stripe. Clicking through those
  roles by hand would test the buttons, not the boundary.
- **The button being hidden for a viewer, and offered to a lead AM on someone
  else's quote** — `recordPayment.viewer.spec.ts` and `recordPayment.am.spec.ts`
  cover exactly that, including that the recorded payment is attributed to the
  person who entered it.
- **The allocation maths** — how money spreads across installments, FIFO order,
  overpayment, partial cover, mixed currencies and signed corrections are all in
  `allocatePayments.test.ts` and `computeAmountDue.test.ts`. The manual items
  above check that the same number reaches three screens, not that the number is
  right.
- **Subtotal and tax per row** — `eventAmounts.test.ts` covers backing a rate out
  of a total, a missing amount, a missing rate, and older rows that predate the
  tax columns.
- **The e-transfer wording, English and French** — asserted in
  `QuotePublicView.test.tsx` (added for this release). Your item above is about
  whether it sits in the right box on the page.
- **The sales-office filter logic and the office currency labels** —
  `filterEvents.test.ts` and `salesOfficeLabel.test.ts`.
- **Blank-draft defaults and the notes prefill** — `quoteValidation.test.ts` and
  `newQuoteNotes.test.ts` cover the placeholder title, the date fallbacks, and
  that a draft in progress is never overwritten.
- **The signature trigger itself** — `payment_does_not_invalidate_signature.test.sql`
  proves in Postgres that a payment no longer moves the contract hash. The item
  on your list is the visible end of that, on a real signed quote.
- **Anything in the driver mobile app** — nothing in this release touches it.

## Permission changes in this release

Three new entries in `permissionPageData.ts`: **QuickBooks Invoice Flag**,
**Payment History** and **Record a Payment**. All three were checked against
`pg_policies` on the live schema, not against the client code.

- `PaymentHistory` INSERT: `admin` and `account_manager` only, and only rows
  marked as manually entered. No UPDATE or DELETE policy exists for anyone.
- `PaymentHistory` SELECT: `admin`, `account_manager`, `viewer` — unchanged.
- A junior account manager is held to their own quotes by the interface, not by
  the database. This is deliberate and written down in the migration and in
  `manual-payment-entry.md`.

**Changed on your instruction:** the **QuickBooks Invoice** checkbox was gated on
quote ownership. It is now gated on role — any admin or account manager can tick
it on any quote — which is what `events_update` already allowed. The matrix and
`payment-accounting-truth.md` §9.1 both say so now.

A fourth entry, **Event Chat**, was added on your instruction. The chat had no
entry at all, and this release changes how it behaves for an event's owner.
